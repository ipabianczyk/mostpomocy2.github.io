# Grafika projektu

Zdjęcie nagłówka: `site/assets/hero.jpg`. Wygenerowano wbudowanym narzędziem ImageGen, bez użycia API/CLI ani klucza użytkownika. Dostarczony zrzut był wzorem kierunku wizualnego; finalna fotografia jest nowym materiałem. Logo: `site/assets/logo.svg`, edytowalna interpretacja motywu mostu i serca.

Prompt użyty dla zdjęcia:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide website hero photograph for Polish social support portal MostPomocy, based on reference screenshot. Create a cinematic natural photograph, wide landscape 16:9 or wider, a woman walking on the left of a person using a manual wheelchair, another man walking on the right, all viewed from behind on a wide wooden pedestrian bridge at sunset over water. Group placed in right half of composition, left half has dark deep navy atmospheric negative space for website text that will be overlaid separately. Sunset golden light in right background, deep blue sky and subtle blue foreground shadows. Warm inclusive hopeful atmosphere, anatomically correct people and wheelchair, understated realistic clothing, no staged stock smiles. No text, no logos, no UI, no typography. Final artwork will be used as hero background in an actual website.
